Workflow

My Creative Blueprint

A systematic, collaborative approach to delivering designs that perfectly align with your vision.

01

Discovery & Research

We start with a thorough discussion about your business, audience, and objectives. Through visual moodboards and audits, we align on a clear strategic direction.

02

Concept & Sketching

I convert thoughts into shapes. I sketch dozens of concepts, selecting the strongest drafts to build into highly precise digital vector wireframes.

03

Refinement

We review the initial proofs. Together, we fine-tune colors, fonts, layouts, and spacings to polish the concept into a bulletproof design.

04

Final Hand-off

You receive complete source file packs ready for digital publication or high-spec print, accompanied by essential usage tips and stylesheets.
